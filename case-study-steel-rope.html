Steel Rope Inspection

Longitudinal guided-wave magnetostrictive inspection

Steel cables and suspension ropes require a dedicated probe arrangement for excitation and reception of longitudinal guided waves. This inspection approach differs from the guided-wave probe systems used for pipelines and pipe supports.

Inspection concept

A magnetostrictive transducer is configured to launch longitudinal guided waves along the rope. Reflected signals can be evaluated for indications associated with changes in the rope structure. Probe configuration and interpretation must account for the rope construction, accessibility, and operating conditions.
