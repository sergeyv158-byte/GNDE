MsSRv5 — Fifth-Generation Pulser/Receiver

Dual-channel instrument for axially symmetric magnetostrictive transduction probes; no 16-channel multiplexer.

Transmitter

  • Two synchronous, differentially driven high-voltage burst outputs
  • Square-wave excitation; 1–14 selectable cycles
  • 1–500 kHz operating frequency
  • Maximum output voltage: 240 Vpp; output current: 40 A peak-to-peak
  • Selectable output amplitude: 0, 25, 50, or 100%
  • Pulse repetition rate: 1, 2, 4, 8, or 16 pulses/s

Receiver

  • Two high-voltage differential inputs; electronic ±90° phase shifting for directionality control
  • Programmable gain: 0–80 dB in 1-dB steps; time-controlled gain: 0–80 dB linear ramp
  • Eight 4-pole active filters: 16, 32, 45, 60, 90, 128, 180, and 250 kHz; other choices available
  • Pulse-echo and pitch-catch modes

MsSRv5M — Sixth-Generation Pulser/Receiver

Instrument with 16-channel relay-type multiplexer for segmented magnetostrictive sensors and arrays.

Transmitter

  • Two synchronous, differentially driven high-voltage burst outputs; in-phase or ±90° phase displacement
  • Square-wave excitation; 1–14 selectable cycles
  • Frequency: 1–500 kHz
  • Maximum output: 240 Vpp and 40 A peak-to-peak
  • Output amplitude: 0, 25, 50, or 100%
  • Pulse repetition rate: 1, 2, 4, 8, or 16 pulses/s

Receiver

  • Two differential inputs with phase adjustment for directionality
  • Gain: 0–80 dB in 1-dB steps; time-controlled gain: 0–80 dB
  • Eight selectable 4-pole active filters: 16, 32, 45, 60, 90, 128, 180, and 250 kHz
  • Pulse-echo and pitch-catch modes

Multiplexer

16-channel relay-type multiplexer, configurable for transmitting and receiving across the 16 sensor channels.

Both instruments are designed and manufactured by Southwest Research Institute (SwRI). Equipment images are used exactly as provided, with no rebuilding, cropping, relabeling, or retouching.
