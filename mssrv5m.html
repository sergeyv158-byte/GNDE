MsSRv5M — Sixth-Generation Pulser/Receiver

Instrumentation designed and manufactured by Southwest Research Institute (SwRI).

A portable magnetostrictive sensor instrumentation system with a 16-channel relay multiplexer, suitable for segmented sensors and guided-wave sensor arrays.

Instrument photograph

Photo supplied for the MsSRv5M; image publication pending asset upload.

Transmitter

  • Two synchronous, differentially driven high-voltage burst outputs, in-phase or ±90° for directionality control
  • Square-wave excitation; 1–14 selectable cycles
  • Maximum output: 240 Vpp; 40 A peak-to-peak
  • Output amplitude: 0, 25, 50 or 100%
  • Frequency: 1–500 kHz
  • Pulse repetition rate: 1, 2, 4, 8 or 16 pulses/s

Receiver

  • Two high-voltage differential inputs, summable with ±90° phase shifting for directionality control
  • Programmable gain: 0–80 dB in 1 dB steps
  • Time-controlled gain: 0–80 dB linear ramp
  • Eight 4-pole active filters: 16, 32, 45, 60, 90, 128, 180 and 250 kHz; other frequencies available
  • Pulse-echo and pitch-catch modes

Multiplexer

16-channel relay-type multiplexer supporting transmit/receive selection among 16 channels.
