Elevated-Temperature Pipe Monitoring

Guided-wave monitoring of pressurized pipe during thermal cycling near 200 °C.

BP-supported test on pressurized pipe

Researchers monitored a 219 mm OD Schedule 40 pipe mockup through 270 days of thermal cycling with a target operating temperature of 200 °C. Figure 5 compares the guided-wave A-scans recorded on day 50 at 190 °C and day 217 at 202 °C.

Sergey Vinogradov and Jay Fisher. Review of Guided Wave Testing Using Magnetostrictive Transducers. 12th European Conference on Non-Destructive Testing (ECNDT), Gothenburg, Sweden, 2018, pp. 4–5.

Related laboratory study of FeCo sensors

Laboratory furnace tests evaluated FeCo patches for generating and receiving guided waves at elevated temperatures. The study documents temperature-dependent signal changes and considerations for high-temperature structural health monitoring.

The test arrangement used spot-welded FeCo patches on a stainless-steel strip waveguide. It provides research background; it does not qualify the current clamp-on pipe sensor or change its published operating-temperature specification.

FeCo furnace-test reference

Vinogradov, S.; Cobb, A.; Fisher, J. Evaluation of FeCo Magnetostrictive Sensors for SHM of Components Operating in Harsh Environmental Conditions. 46th Annual Review of Progress in Quantitative Nondestructive Evaluation, 2019. QNDE2019-1234.
