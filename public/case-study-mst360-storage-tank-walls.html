GNDE · Case Study

MsT 360 Storage Tank Wall Corrosion Screen

A servo motor rotates the directional probe head in angular steps to scan the tank wall from a single position.

Tank wall inspection

The scanner records a guided-wave response at each angle. In the tank inspection shown on ICENDE slide 25, the rotating head advanced in 5° steps and collected data at 60, 70, and 80 kHz. Several indications matched known corrosion areas on the tank shell.

EPRI BOP 2025 · MsT 360 tank-wall inspection

Sergey Vinogradov and Jay Fisher. Corrosion Mapping in Storage Tank Walls, Bottoms and Service Water Piping Using Guided Wave Magnetostrictive Transducers. Balance of Plant NDE and Performance Program Symposium, Minneapolis, Minnesota, July 28–31, 2025.

Slide 13 shows the probe on the tank and the corrosion map from the rotating-head scan. Slide 14 shows how welds and other vessel features are marked on the scan and how individual indications are reported by position.
