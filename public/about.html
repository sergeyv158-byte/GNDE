About Guided NDE LLC

Guided NDE LLC (GNDE) was founded in 2008 by Dr. Sergey A. Vinogradov, inventor of patented magnetostrictive guided-wave transducer designs based on the reversed Wiedemann effect.

Since its founding, Guided NDE (GNDE) has designed, manufactured, and supplied specialized magnetostrictive sensors and related equipment to Southwest Research Institute (SwRI). This collaboration has supported advanced research, accelerated the development and scaling of innovative inspection technologies, and enabled their use across a broad range of industrial inspection challenges.

In October 2026, GNDE entered into a licensing agreement with Southwest Research Institute (SwRI) to further commercialize magnetostrictive sensor technology, expand its availability to a broader range of industrial clients, and support wider adoption of guided-wave inspection solutions.
