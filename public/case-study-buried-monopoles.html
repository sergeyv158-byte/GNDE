Buried Monopole Inspection

Multifrequency guided-wave imaging of a corroded polygonal steel monopole using a four-element MsT array.

Inspection and result

Full matrix capture and TFM processing were used to image the pole at multiple frequencies. The 64 kHz and 180 kHz measurements provided complementary responses to regions with severe and more moderate wall loss.

Damage nearer the array can reduce the response from more distant reflectors. Image amplitudes therefore require interpretation together with propagation distance, attenuation, geometry, and frequency.
