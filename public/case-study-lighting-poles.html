Lighting Pole Inspection

Separate examples address lighting poles embedded in soil and concrete.

Lighting pole embedded in soil

A four-segment MsT array inspected a 102 mm outside-diameter lighting pole. At 180 kHz, TFM showed the corrosion region near the soil line with greater spatial localization than the corresponding B-scan; the pole-end response remained visible.

Lighting pole embedded in concrete

Measurements at 180 and 250 kHz demonstrated the influence of the steel–concrete interface on guided-wave reflections and attenuation. The strong interface response is not by itself evidence of corrosion.
