CUP Application. Axial and circumferential scans

An axial scan screened for corrosion under two concrete pipe supports. A follow-up circumferential scan mapped the affected area beneath support 2.

Axial and circumferential scan results

An 11-segment MsT bracelet scanned axially along the 42-inch OD carbon-steel pipe, which had a 0.4-inch wall. At support 2, an 8 × 8 MsT array scanned around the pipe and located a corrosion cluster approximately 20 inches (0.5 m) from the probe.

Source

Sergey Vinogradov and Jay Fisher. Case Studies of Guided Wave Testing Using Magnetostrictive Transducer Arrays. ASNT Annual Conference, Columbus, Ohio, October 2026.
