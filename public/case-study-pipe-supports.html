Corrosion Inspection Beneath Pipe Supports

Axial and circumferential guided-wave scans of a carbon-steel pipe at concrete supports.

Inspection configuration

The source example uses a 42 in (1.07 m) outside-diameter pipe with a 0.4 in (10 mm) wall. An eleven-segment MsT bracelet provided axial scans at two concrete supports.

A suspect region beneath support 2 was examined with an 8 × 8 MsT array positioned for circumferential scanning. This documented example uses the eleven-segment bracelet and the 8 × 8 array.

Inspection result

The follow-up circumferential scan identified a corrosion cluster approximately 0.5 m (20 in) from the probe. The source images show an axial scan at 128 kHz and a circumferential scan at 180 kHz.
