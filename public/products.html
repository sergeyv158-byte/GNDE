CHAPTER 01 · PIPELINE INSPECTION

Pipelines and Pipe Supports

Multi-segment magnetostrictive guided-wave probes for axial and circumferential pipe inspection, including regions beneath pipe supports. Six probe configurations from the prior approved layout.

PROBE 01

8-Segment MsT Probes

Designed for pipes with nominal diameters of 4–6 inches. Suitable for installation where approximately 25 mm of clearance is available between adjacent pipes.

PROBE 02

Axial and Circumferential Inspection

The 9-segment MsT bracelet probe wraps around the pipe for axial scanning; the 8 × 8 MsT array is a flat probe mounted along the pipe surface.

PROBE 03

Low-Profile 8-Segment MsT Probes

Designed for inspection in restricted spaces, requiring approximately 11 mm clearance between adjacent pipes.

PROBE 04

11-Segment Bracelet MsT Probe

Originally designed for a 10-inch pipe, shown installed on a 55-inch OD pipe to perform axial scanning of pipe supports.

PROBE 05

9-Segment MsT Bracelet — Full and Partial Coverage

9-segment bracelet probe configurations demonstrating full and partial circumferential coverage, including installation using a clamping strap.

PROBE 06

4-Segment Adjustable MsT Bracelet Probe — 102 mm OD

Designed for pipes starting at 102 mm outside diameter, with partial-coverage configurations extending to approximately 170 mm.

Installation and acoustic coupling: Bracelet-type segmented MsT probes wrap around the pipe. Inflatable bladders can conform individual segments to the pipe surface; an appropriate couplant improves acoustic coupling where required by the design.

General probe specifications: Operating frequencies approximately 20–200 kHz. Probe weights approximately 1–5 lb (0.45–2.27 kg), depending on size and configuration. A probe sized for a 16-inch OD pipe is approximately 5 lb. Array configurations support TFM imaging with compatible acquisition electronics.

CHAPTER 02 · STORAGE TANK INSPECTION

Storage Tank Walls and Bottoms

Magnetostrictive array probes for guided-wave screening and imaging of tank shell walls and tank bottoms, including areas that are difficult to reach with conventional point-by-point UT.

CONFIGURATION 01

8 × 8 MsT Array — Axial Tank Wall Scan

8 × 8 MsT array clamped for axial scanning of storage tank walls. Operates with the MSSRV5M instrument.

CONFIGURATION 02

8 × 8 MsT Array — Tank Bottom Inspection

Segmented 8 × 8 MsT array with clamping fixture for guided-wave screening of tank bottoms. Operates with the MSSRV5M instrument. The array probe can be mounted on a tank-bottom extension as short as 13 mm (approximately 0.51 in).

CONFIGURATION 03

MsT 360 — Omnidirectional Tank Wall Inspection

Omnidirectional MsT 360 probe with rotating probe head for storage tank wall inspection. Controlled by the MsSRv5 unit.

Inspection principle: Guided-wave measurements provide wide-area screening; focused follow-up UT can quantify local wall thickness at selected indications. The 8 × 8 array is a flat surface-mounted probe, while MsT 360 uses a rotating omnidirectional probe assembly.

Image review: the MsT 360 image is a retouched visual draft; verify the probe assembly appearance against the original photograph before publishing.

CHAPTER 04 · INTERNAL TUBING INSPECTION

Heat Exchanger and Tubing

Internal-diameter (ID)-coupled magnetostrictive probes for rapid guided-wave screening. The probes generate torsional guided-wave modes in tubes over a frequency range of 30–150 kHz, depending on tube conditions.

FIGURE 01 · TWO HEAT EXCHANGER PROBES / THREE REPLACEABLE HEADS

Two Heat Exchanger Probes and Three Replaceable Probe Heads

Symmetric transduction (not TFM) for internal guided-wave screening. An expandable probe head and inflatable bladder provide dry acoustic contact with the tube wall.

Applicable tube ID range: approximately 11–50 mm (0.43–1.97 inches), using appropriately sized interchangeable probe heads.

Each head can expand to accommodate a tube ID up to 1 mm above its nominal fit ID.

FIGURE 02 · FOUR-SEGMENT TFM PROBE

Expandable Four-Segment TFM Probe Head

For internal inspection of 4-inch nominal Schedule 40 pipe, with an inside diameter of 4.026 in (102.26 mm). The probe body is displayed horizontally with its pistol-grip handle pointing down.

Acoustic coupling: Apply suitable shear-wave couplant to the expandable segments before inserting the probe to achieve reliable coupling against the internal pipe wall.

Compatible materials: Metallic tubes, including magnetic and non-magnetic alloys, carbon steel, stainless steel, and other compatible metal alloys. Inspection performance is application-dependent.

Surface preparation: Clean at least 2 inches (50.8 mm) of accessible tube length past the tube sheet to remove rust and deposits in the probe-coupling area before inspection.
