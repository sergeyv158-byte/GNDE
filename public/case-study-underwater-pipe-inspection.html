Underwater Pipe Inspection

The team developed and tested a claw-type magnetostrictive (MsT) probe for underwater inspection of piping in nuclear fuel pools. This nuclear application required halogen-free materials for both the probe cabling and the housing.

Deploying a bracelet MsT probe for subsea pipe inspection will require testing the probe at Southwest Research Institute’s deepwater test facility.
