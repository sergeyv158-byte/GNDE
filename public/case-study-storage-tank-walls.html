Storage Tank Wall Corrosion Screening

Guided-wave imaging of corrosion in a water-storage tank shell, including a region beneath a welded tank support.

Inspection and result

An MsT array mapped corrosion clusters in the tank shell. Follow-up phased-array ultrasonic testing of a selected region confirmed wall loss up to 85%.

The study also examined the shell beneath a welded tank support. This example concerns a support attached to a storage tank; the separate pipe-support case is listed under Pipelines & Pipe Supports.
