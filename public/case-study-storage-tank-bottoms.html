Storage Tank Bottom Corrosion Mapping

Guided-wave screening of a lap-welded tank floor from the external tank-bottom extension.

Inspection and result

An MsT array mounted on the accessible tank-bottom extension collected data used to map and rank suspect corrosion within the floor.

Measurements at multiple frequencies and complementary wall scans helped distinguish bottom indications from tank-wall responses. The study reports screening and relative severity assessment; confirmation and sizing require suitable follow-up inspection.
