Tubing inspection from the tube OD

Inspection of small-diameter tubing from the OD using a claw-type MsT probe

A claw-type magnetostrictive (MsT) probe couples to the outside diameter (OD) of small-diameter tubing for guided-wave inspection.

Presentation reference: ICENDE 2023 keynote, slide 22 — MsT for GWT of Boiler Tubes.

Multi-frequency guided-wave inspection of boiler tubes

With the magnetostrictive transducer (MsT) in place, guided-wave measurements were collected from two boiler tubes without changing coil adaptors. The figure shows a set of measurements at 20, 32, 45, 64, and 90 kHz.

Related probe: OD-coupled tubing inspection with a claw-type MsT probe.

Results

Pipe 1. Reflections from two welds and a pipe support provide reference points for comparing the traces at different frequencies.

Pipe 2. A suspect indication appears at about 20 ft (6 m) from the probe. It is most apparent in the 20 kHz trace. Its cause and dimensions were not established.

Why multiple frequencies matter

Lower frequencies (20–50 kHz) are useful for screening for generalized corrosion. Higher frequencies (60–90 kHz) can help detect localized pitting and examine regions beneath pipe supports. The presentation also identifies 180–250 kHz for these applications. Comparing measurements from the same probe position shows which indications are most apparent at each frequency.
