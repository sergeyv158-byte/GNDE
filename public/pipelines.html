Pipelines and Pipe Supports

Multi-segment magnetostrictive guided-wave probes for axial and circumferential pipe inspection, including regions beneath pipe supports. Six probe configurations from the prior approved layout.

8-Segment MsT Probes

Designed for pipes with nominal diameters of 4–6 inches. Suitable for installation where approximately 25 mm of clearance is available between adjacent pipes.

Axial and Circumferential Inspection

The 9-segment MsT bracelet probe wraps around the pipe for axial scanning; the 8 × 8 MsT array is a flat probe mounted along the pipe surface.

Low-Profile 8-Segment MsT Probes

Designed for inspection in restricted spaces, requiring approximately 11 mm clearance between adjacent pipes.

11-Segment Bracelet MsT Probe

Originally designed for a 10-inch pipe, shown installed on a 55-inch OD pipe to perform axial scanning of pipe supports.

9-Segment MsT Bracelet — Full and Partial Coverage

9-segment bracelet probe configurations demonstrating full and partial circumferential coverage, including installation using a clamping strap.

4-Segment Adjustable MsT Bracelet Probe — 102 mm OD

Designed for pipes starting at 102 mm outside diameter, with partial-coverage configurations extending to approximately 170 mm.

Installation and acoustic coupling: Bracelet-type segmented MsT probes wrap around the pipe. Inflatable bladders can conform individual segments to the pipe surface; an appropriate couplant improves acoustic coupling where required by the design.

General probe specifications: Operating frequencies approximately 20–200 kHz. Probe weights approximately 1–5 lb (0.45–2.27 kg), depending on size and configuration. A probe sized for a 16-inch OD pipe is approximately 5 lb. Array configurations support TFM imaging with compatible acquisition electronics.
