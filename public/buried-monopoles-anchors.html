Four-Segment MsT Probe with Junction Box for Flat Anchors

Four-segment MsT probe for flat anchors and multisided poles. Specifically designed for low-frequency inspection and particularly suitable for composite structures. Frequency range: 20–90 kHz. Combined aperture: 16 inches. Instrument: MsSRv5M. TFM imaging.

TFM capability: Multi-segment MsT probe configurations are used with the MsSRv5M instrument for TFM imaging. Single-segment and split-ring MsT probe designs use symmetric guided-wave interrogation with the MsSRv5 instrument.
