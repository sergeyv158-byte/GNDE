Heat Exchanger Tube Inspection

Internal guided-wave inspection of heat exchanger tubing.

Oil-cooler tube screening at a nuclear power plant

Inspectors screened 329 SeaCure® tubes with a 100 kHz torsional guided-wave probe. They identified relevant indications in 21 tubes. Four of those tubes were pulled for destructive examination. The examination confirmed anomalies in three tubes and traced the fourth indication to a tube support plate.
