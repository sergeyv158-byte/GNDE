MsSRv5 — Fifth-Generation Pulser/Receiver

Instrumentation designed and manufactured by Southwest Research Institute (SwRI).

A portable guided-wave pulser/receiver for magnetostrictive sensors, without the 16-channel multiplexer.

Instrument photograph

Photograph supplied on page 1 of the SwRI MsSRv5 brochure; image publication pending extraction and asset upload.

Transmitter

  • Two synchronous differentially driven high-voltage burst outputs
  • Square wave; 1–14 selectable cycles
  • Maximum 240 Vpp and 40 A peak-to-peak
  • Amplitude settings: 0, 25, 50, 100%
  • Frequency range: 1–500 kHz
  • Pulse repetition rates: 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16 pulses/s

Receiver

  • Two high-voltage differential inputs with electronic ±90° phase shift before summation
  • Programmable gain: 0–80 dB in 1 dB steps
  • Time-controlled gain: 0–80 dB linear ramp
  • Eight 4-pole active filters: 16, 32, 45, 60, 90, 128, 180 and 250 kHz (standard)
  • Pulse-echo and pitch-catch operation

Source: Southwest Research Institute, MsSRv5 Fifth-Generation Magnetostrictive Sensor Instrumentation System brochure, pp. 1–2.
