Vibration Magnetostrictive Sensor

Flexible magnetostrictive (MsT) sensor element with a ferromagnetic strip and winding, paired with a flexible magnetic belt for shear vibration sensing.

Sensor specifications

Frequency rangeApproximately 5–500 kHz
Resistance2–20 Ω, depending on element length and operating frequency
Sensor element thicknessApproximately 1 mm
Total thickness with magnetic belt2–3.5 mm, depending on configuration
Available lengths1–24 inches (25.4–609.6 mm)
Available nominal strip widths0.5 inch (12.7 mm), 0.75 inch (19.05 mm), and 1 inch (25.4 mm)
Overall width with coatingApproximately 13.1 mm for the 0.5-inch strip
Surface conformityFlat surfaces and 3-inch-diameter pipe (76.2 mm), with custom configurations for smaller radii
Nominal operating temperatureUp to approximately 80 °C
Custom temperature configurationsUp to 200 °C

Custom packaging and permanent monitoring

These sensor elements can be integrated into custom packages, including segmented collars with multichannel data acquisition, for permanent structural health monitoring and other applications.

Published sensor design and performance

Section 2 of the Sensors paper (pages 2–5, Figures 1–3) describes the ferromagnetic strip, surrounding coil, magnetic belt, and flexible encapsulation. It explains how strip geometry affects unwanted wave modes and sideways radiation.

Section 6 (page 9, Figure 9) reports a 64 kHz test using an eight-segment probe on a 7 mm carbon-steel plate. The plate-end reflection achieved a signal-to-noise ratio of 53.9 dB. Sideways SH0 reflections reached 1.7% of the plate-end amplitude; mode-converted corner responses reached 3%. These results describe the published test configuration.

Vinogradov, S.; Akimov, N.; Cobb, A.; Fisher, J. Screening of Corrosion in Storage Tank Walls and Bottoms Using an Array of Guided Wave Magnetostrictive Transducers. Sensors 2026, 26(4), 1253.
