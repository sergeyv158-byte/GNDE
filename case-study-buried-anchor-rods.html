Guided-Wave Testing of Buried Anchor Rods Using Magnetostrictive Transducers

Case study based on the supplied slide titled “GWT of Buried Anchors using MsTs.” A magnetostrictive transducer (MsT) is installed on the accessible section of a rod to send guided waves toward its buried end and record reflections from changes along the structure.

Inspection configuration

The inspected anchors were approximately 2 inches (51 mm) in outside diameter and included a concrete attachment near the bottom. The slide illustrates the anchor surrounded by soil, a concrete region at its far end, the MsT probe, and a weldment near the accessible end.

Comparison of inspection results

  • Anchor (b): A clear bottom/end reflection was observed, and no other relevant indications were identified.
  • Anchor (c): The end reflection was absent, and multiple indications appeared in the middle section. The slide states that the condition was visually identified as a sharp bend; the displayed indications are marked as a corrosion region.

Interpretation and limitations

The technique provides a qualitative indication of rod condition. The slide proposes ranking anchors into good, medium, and bad condition categories. The measurements shown do not establish a numerical percentage of corrosion wall loss or a quantitative remaining-strength assessment.
